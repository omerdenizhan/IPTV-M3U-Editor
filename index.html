M3U & M3U8 Editor

Free, private, local, browser-based M3U & M3U8 playlist editor.
Drop .m3u or .m3u8 files anywhere to open them. Multiple files are merged. Ctrl/Cmd+K focuses search.
Edit the first line of the playlist here. EPG and global catchup attributes are preserved when loading and downloading. Don't touch it if you don't know what it is.
